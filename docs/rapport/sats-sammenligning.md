# SATS: our report against the DNB Markets note, page by page

Phase 3 of the report-generator mission (2 November 2026). Our document is `examples/SATS/report/SATS_Research_Report_2026-11-02.pdf`
(25 pages, built from the rebuilt workbook after the Q3 2026 report of 27 October 2026, share price NOK 43.95 at the
30 October close). The reference is DNB Markets' 45-page note of 7 March 2025 (`docs/rapport/struktur.md`). The two documents
are eighteen months apart and of different kinds – theirs a pre-results and CMD preview with half its pages on
self-collected data, ours a post-results update – so the comparison is about what each section achieves, not about the
numbers in it.

Legend: **=** we cover it as well, **+** we cover it better, **−** we cover it less well, **×** we cannot cover it and say why.

## 2. What we cover as well

The spine of the report: front page, investment case, company overview, quarter preview against public consensus,
estimate revisions, valuation by several methods and the full statements. Every exhibit in ours carries its source and the
date of the data, as theirs does.

## 3. What we cover better

1. **The link between the view and the model.** Scenarios with probabilities, stories, triggers and signposts; the scenario
   paths by year; kill criteria with thresholds, latest actuals and status. Theirs has bull and bear values built as ±4 %
   revenue and a multiple.
2. **What the market prices in.** The reverse DCF solved driver by driver, next to our base case and the latest actual, and
   the case gate that refuses a view that does not differ from the market. Theirs values on a single P/E multiple.
3. **Estimate revisions that cannot drift.** Produced from the stored previous build, with the valuation then and now.
4. **What we do not know.** The data map, the ranked what-ifs, the stress tests and the open questions – the page that says
   where the case is weakest.
5. **Verification on the record.** The build gate, the workbook checks and the audit are printed; the report is not built
   if they fail.

## 4. What we cannot cover, and why

| Item | Why not | What we do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Consensus for every P&L line and segment, quarterly and annual | Infront/Bloomberg/FactSet are paid | The company-published Modular Finance table (revenue, EBITDA, EBITDA before IFRS 16, net profit, members; Q4 2026E–2027E) and Yahoo's EPS mean; every other cell says *not available* |
| 12-month forward P/E history and peer-group multiple history | Bloomberg | Trailing multiples at year-end prices from the company's own figures, labelled |
| Peer operating comparison (clubs, members, revenue per member, opex per club, estimates) | Visible Alpha | Peer multiples only; the peers' own reports would give history and are listed as a collection task |
| Web traffic for SATS and the industry | Similarweb | Listed as missing; no free equivalent |
| Largest shareholders | The company page loads its table dynamically; the annual-report list was not retrieved in this edition | Stated in the data map; the Q3 report's share count and treasury shares are used |
| Price tracker 2019–2026 and competitor screens with a visit date | Not collected yet | First-edition observations reused with their dates; marked as not re-collected |
| Target-vs-delivery bridges from the CMD, multi-year KPI series since 2018, club maps | Collection work, not template work | The quarterly store covers Q4 2024–Q3 2026; the exhibit types exist |
